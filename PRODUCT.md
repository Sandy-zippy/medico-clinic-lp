# Medico landing page (impeccable context)

register: brand

## Product purpose
One landing page, four keyword-keyed variants (?v=medical|dental|optometry|pharmacy), receiving Google Search
traffic from Medico Construction and Design's clinic design-build ad groups. Its only job is a qualified form
submission that carries the gclid. Speed is the brand decision: the client's site loads in 18.6 s and Google rates
its landing page experience below average.

## Users
Clinic owners in BC and Alberta with a unit to build or renovate: physicians, dentists, optometrists, vets,
pharmacy owners. 33 of 49 recent enquiries were new clinic buildouts; 17 already held a location; 15 did not know
their budget. They write like this: "2100 sq ft clinic, looking to improve wait room, kitchen, physicians office
and add 1 treatment room." "I am on my own in this project, but with right guidance, I can make it."

## Brand and tone
Plain, trade-credible, specific. Medico's own lines: "Design, permit and build under one roof." "Renovate without
closing doors." Never agency voice. Banned: trusted, expert, leading, quality, professional, your partner,
one team, concept to completion. No em dashes. No prices. No guarantee.

## Anti-references
The client's own WordPress site (41 stylesheets, hero JPEG 1.79 MB). Generic "healthcare = white + teal" SaaS
pages. Stock-photo hero banners. Big-number hero-metric rows.

## Strategic principles
1. Message match: H1 continues the ad group's query echo.
2. The form is the hero visual; everything else exists to earn the submission.
3. Every claim traces to the offer doc or the client's published copy.
