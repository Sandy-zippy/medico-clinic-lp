Questions animal hospital owners ask

How much does it cost to build a vet clinic?

It depends on the space, its existing plumbing and power, how many exam, treatment and ward areas you need, imaging, and finishes. A site assessment is the fastest way to get a real number for your space, not a guess.

How big should a vet clinic be?

Big enough for your exam rooms, treatment, surgery, wards, imaging and staff areas, with room for how your team moves between them. We lay it out around your workflow from the first plan.

Should I talk to Medico before signing a lease?

Yes. Drains, power and ventilation decide where treatment, surgery and wards can go. We can flag problems before you commit to a space.

Can you renovate a clinic that's still operating?

Some projects can be phased to reduce disruption. We assess access, safety and sequencing first and plan the right approach with you.
