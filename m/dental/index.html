Questions dental office owners ask

How much does it cost to build a dental office?

It depends on the space, the number of operatories, your equipment, imaging, the mechanical room and finishes. A site assessment is the fastest way to get a real number for your space, not a guess.

When should I pick my equipment?

Early. Chairs, compressors, vacuum and imaging decide where plumbing and power go. We plan the rooms around the equipment you choose.

Should I talk to Medico before signing a lease?

Yes. Floor drains, slab work, power and ventilation can change your layout and cost. We can flag problems before you commit to a space.

Can you renovate a practice that's still operating?

Some projects can be phased to reduce disruption. We assess access, safety and sequencing first and plan the right approach with you.
