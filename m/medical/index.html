What owners ask us first

How much does it cost to build a medical clinic?

It depends on the unit, the number of exam rooms, its plumbing and power, accessibility work and finishes. A site assessment is how you get a real number for your unit, not a guess.

How many exam rooms fit in my unit?

It depends on the shape of the unit, where plumbing can go and how patients move through. We plan it with you before you commit.

Should I talk to Medico before signing a lease?

Yes. Plumbing, power, accessible washrooms and permits can change your layout, cost and opening date. We can flag problems before you sign.

Can you renovate a clinic that's still operating?

Some projects can be phased to reduce disruption. We assess access, safety and sequencing first and plan the right approach with you.

Why isn't there a price on this page?

Because a clinic's cost is set by the unit you have, not by a list. After the site assessment you get a real number for your unit.
