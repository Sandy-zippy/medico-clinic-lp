Questions optometry clinic owners ask

How much does it cost to build an optometry clinic?

It depends on the space, the number of lanes, your equipment, the dispensary and finishes. A site assessment is the fastest way to get a real number for your space, not a guess.

How long does an exam lane need to be?

It depends on your equipment and whether you use mirrors or a screen. We check it against your space before you commit.

Should I talk to Medico before signing a lease?

Yes. Lane length, power and storefront decide whether a unit works. We can flag problems before you commit to a space.

Can you renovate a clinic that's still operating?

Some projects can be phased to reduce disruption. We assess access, safety and sequencing first and plan the right approach with you.
