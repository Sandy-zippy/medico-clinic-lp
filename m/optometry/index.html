Where optometry builds go wrong

Three problems we check for on every optometry site assessment, shown in an exam lane we built.

  1. 1
    Lanes too short for your equipment

    We measure lane length against your chart, mirrors or screen before you sign.

  2. 2
    Instruments with no power or room

    We place power, data and room for your pretest and exam instruments.

  3. 3
    No plumbing for the edger

    We plan sinks and plumbing for your lab and exam rooms.

What owners ask us first

How much does it cost to build an optometry clinic?

It depends on the unit, the number of lanes, your equipment, the dispensary and finishes. A site assessment is how you get a real number for your unit, not a guess.

How long does an exam lane need to be?

It depends on your equipment and whether you use mirrors or a screen. We check it against your unit before you commit.

Should I talk to Medico before signing a lease?

Yes. Lane length, power and storefront decide whether a unit works. We can flag problems before you commit to a unit.

Can you renovate a clinic that's still operating?

Some projects can be phased to reduce disruption. We assess access, safety and sequencing first and plan the right approach with you.

Why isn't there a price on this page?

Because a clinic's cost is set by the unit you have, not by a list. After the site assessment you get a real number for your unit.
